Need help with Veylo?

Contact support for account access, privacy requests, deletion issues, bug reports, or questions about campus status, friends, feed, and messages.

Contact

Email: support@veylo.app

For an account request, include the email address used for your Veylo account and a short description. For a technical problem, include your device model, app version, platform, and operating system version if available. Do not send your password, sign-in codes, or unrelated private information about other people.

Account Or Data Deletion

Start in-app deletion from Profile > Settings > Delete account. If you cannot access the app, use our public account-deletion request page.

Safety And Moderation

Use the in-app report and block controls for safety issues involving profiles, feed posts, chats, or messages. You can also email support about account, privacy, or moderation concerns. Veylo support is not an emergency service; contact local emergency services if someone is in immediate danger.

Veylo is for university students in Australia aged 18 or older. Email support with an age-related account concern or to request review of an account decision. Do not send identity documents, passwords, or sign-in codes.

Report A Problem

You can email support or use Settings > Beta feedback, the in-app feedback form. The form sends your message, selected severity, optional screen name, and technical details including app version, platform, and operating system version. For account deletion, use the deletion instructions above.
