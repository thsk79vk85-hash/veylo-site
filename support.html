Need help with Veylo?

Contact support for account access, privacy requests, TestFlight issues, bug reports, or questions about campus status, friends, feed, and messages.

Account Or Data Deletion

To request account or data deletion, email support with the subject "Veylo data deletion". We will use the account email address to verify the request.

Beta Feedback

If you are testing Veylo through TestFlight, you can also send feedback through Apple's TestFlight app. For account-specific requests, email support directly.
