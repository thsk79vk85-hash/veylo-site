Support

Need help with Veylo?

Contact support for account access, privacy requests, deletion issues, TestFlight issues, bug reports, or questions about campus status, friends, feed, and messages.

Contact

Email: support@veylo.app

Please include the email address used for your Veylo account, your device model, iOS version, and a short description of the issue.

Account Or Data Deletion

You can delete your account directly in the app from Profile > Delete account. If you cannot access the app, use our public account-deletion request page.

Beta Feedback

If you are testing Veylo through TestFlight, you can also send feedback through Apple's TestFlight app. For account-specific requests, email support directly.
