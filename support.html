Need help with Veylo?

Contact support for account access, privacy requests, deletion issues, beta access, bug reports, or questions about campus status, friends, feed, and messages.

Contact

Email: support@veylo.app

Please include the email address used for your Veylo account, your device model, app version, platform, operating system version, and a short description of the issue.

Account Or Data Deletion

You can delete your account directly in the app from Profile > Delete account. If you cannot access the app, use our public account-deletion request page.

Safety And Moderation

Use the in-app report and block controls for safety issues involving profiles, feed posts, chats, or messages. For urgent account or privacy concerns, email support directly.

Beta Feedback

If you are testing Veylo through TestFlight, you can also send feedback through Apple's TestFlight app. Android beta testers should use the provided beta feedback channel. For account-specific requests, email support directly.
