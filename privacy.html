Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Veylo collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when students use the Veylo campus social app and related support pages.

Who Operates Veylo

Veylo is operated by John Tawadros, an individual based in Queensland, Australia. In this Privacy Policy, “Veylo”, “we”, “us”, and “our” refer to John Tawadros as the operator of the Veylo service.

Information We Collect

Veylo may collect the following information when you use the app:

  • Account information, including email address, password authentication handled by Supabase, authentication and session identifiers, and records of acceptance of our terms and privacy policy.
  • Profile information, including first name, last name, display name, username, university, selected campuses, degree or course, year level, optional student email and verification status, and optional major or specialisation selections or self-reported text.
  • Campus status information, including general campus, zone label, visibility choice, status text, last-confirmed and expiry times, freshness preferences, reminder processing records, and automatic-tracking verification times.
  • Social information, including friend requests, accepted friend connections, group names and membership, messages and read state, feed posts, feed comments, reactions, report submissions, moderation records, block lists, and related timestamps.
  • Profile photo information, including pending profile photo submissions and approved public avatar URLs.
  • Notification information, including Expo push tokens, device type, token status, last-seen times, notification preferences, and in-app notification records.
  • Optional support and feedback information, including your message, the screen you identify, severity, app version, platform, operating system version, app ownership, and execution-environment details when you use the in-app Beta feedback form; course suggestions and account-deletion requests when you submit them.

Location And Campus Presence

Veylo uses device location only when you choose a location-based campus status feature. Manual campus checks and the optional check while the app is open use foreground location permission. With the app-open option enabled, Veylo can use a current location result to confirm or clear campus presence when you return to the app. Opening the app alone is not proof that you are still on campus. If you enable always-on automatic campus tracking, Veylo also requests background location permission and registers campus geofences so the operating system may notify the app when your device enters or leaves a supported campus area while the app is in the background. Background behaviour depends on your device, operating system, permission settings, and platform limitations.

To determine campus presence, the app obtains coordinates from the device and compares them on the device with configured campus zones. A manual check may use a recent last-known location that is no more than approximately five minutes old when a fresh position is unavailable. A freshness check on returning to the app requires a current location result; an unavailable result does not silently reconfirm presence. In the normal campus-status flow, Veylo sends its backend only the derived result, such as on-campus or off-campus, a campus or zone label, and related status times. Veylo does not intentionally send or store exact GPS coordinates in its backend as part of that normal flow. Your device operating system and Apple or Google location services may process exact location information to provide the location result under their own terms and privacy practices.

A campus or zone label still reveals location information, sometimes within a relatively small area. It is linked to your account. Campus status is an indication of presence, not a live map or a guarantee that someone is still at that location.

You can turn off automatic campus tracking in Veylo and can revoke location permission in your device settings. Turning it off stops future automatic checks, but it does not by itself erase a campus status already stored in Veylo. You can update or hide that status using Veylo’s privacy controls or delete your account.

Campus Status Freshness And Reminders

Campus freshness is enabled by default. For a status that is not maintained by verified always-on tracking, Veylo normally schedules a reminder three hours after the latest confirmation and stops showing that status as current after four hours, or at midnight in Brisbane, whichever comes first. A reminder is not scheduled beyond that expiry. App-open location checks do not turn reminders off while the app is closed. Verified always-on tracking can suppress these reminders; platform or permission failures can prevent automatic checks from occurring.

You can confirm that you are still there, say that you have left, or dismiss a reminder. Dismissing it does not extend your status. Turning off freshness reminders removes the normal three-hour reminder and four-hour expiry, but the Brisbane midnight reset still applies. These controls are separate from location permission and automatic tracking.

Expiry or leaving removes current campus presence; it does not necessarily erase the last confirmed campus and time. That information may remain visible as past activity to people allowed by your privacy settings. Veylo stores the confirmation, expiry and reminder state needed to keep this distinction and avoid duplicate reminders.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide app functionality, including:

  • Creating and managing accounts.
  • Helping users choose university, campus, degree, and optional study details.
  • Showing campus status to the audience allowed by your privacy controls.
  • Supporting friend discovery, friend requests, messages, group chats, and campus feed posts.
  • Reviewing profile photo submissions before they become public profile pictures.
  • Sending optional push notifications for friend requests, messages, reactions, and campus status alerts when you allow them.
  • Maintaining security, preventing abuse, responding to reports, debugging issues, and improving reliability.

Storage And Service Providers

Veylo uses Supabase for authentication, database storage, file storage, and related backend services. Supabase stores the account, profile, privacy, safety, campus status, feed, notification, and messaging records needed for the app to function. Expo processes push notification tokens and payloads for delivery. Apple Push Notification service and Google’s Firebase Cloud Messaging may then deliver those notifications to devices. Apple, Google, and the operating system may also provide native platform and location services. Veylo’s public legal and support pages are hosted using GitHub Pages. Expo also provides app-update delivery. These services receive the network and technical information needed to serve requests. Email providers process messages you send to support.

We do not sell personal information or use it for cross-app advertising tracking. The current app does not include third-party advertising SDKs. Connections to Veylo’s backend use encrypted transport. Messages are stored on the backend; they are not end-to-end encrypted.

Push Notifications And Notification Content

If you enable notifications, Veylo stores the information needed to address and manage them, including an Expo push token, device type, whether the token is enabled, its last-seen time, an app-generated installation identifier, cryptographic binding proof, account association, and your notification preferences. Veylo may also store an in-app notification record containing the recipient, actor, title, body, routing information, read time, and related timestamps.

Depending on the alert, a push notification may include a sender or actor name, a group name, a campus-status event or status text, or a preview of a message of up to approximately 80 characters. A status alert can describe an activity such as being free or studying, or say that a friend is at your campus when permitted by their privacy settings. It may also include account, message, conversation, or event identifiers and links needed to open the relevant screen. A campus freshness reminder can name the campus you last confirmed and offer actions to confirm your presence or say you have left. This content can also appear on your lock screen, depending on your device settings. Veylo sends this payload through the Expo Push Service, which forwards it to Apple Push Notification service or Google’s Firebase Cloud Messaging. Those providers process the token and notification content for delivery and delivery troubleshooting.

Depending on the recipient’s device settings, notification content may appear on a lock screen and may be visible to anyone who can view that device. You can disable notification categories in Veylo or disable all Veylo notifications in your device settings. Disabling notifications controls future delivery but does not retract a notification already delivered or remove information already stored on another user’s device or in their Veylo notification history.

Website Visits And Analytics

Our public website includes Vercel Web Analytics. On pages where the analytics script loads, it measures visits using information such as the page URL, referring page, visit time, approximate region, and browser, operating system, and device type. We use these statistics to understand use of the website. Website hosts also process network request information, including IP addresses, to serve pages.

This website measurement is separate from the native Veylo app. Vercel describes its default analytics as using a request-derived visitor hash without third-party cookies. See Vercel’s analytics privacy information for its processing practices. Do not include passwords, sign-in codes, or private account information in website URLs.

Veylo is operated from Australia, but some service providers process or store information overseas. Push notification information may be disclosed to or processed by Expo, Apple, Google, and their service providers in the United States and other countries in which they operate. GitHub and Vercel may also process standard website request information, such as an IP address and browser or device information, when you visit Veylo’s public legal or support pages.

Privacy and data-access laws in those countries may differ from Australian law. We limit information sent to each provider to what is reasonably required for the relevant service and use the access controls and security features available to us. Provider infrastructure, subprocessors, and processing locations can change. You can contact us for current information about the providers and overseas locations known to Veylo.

Sharing

Profile and campus-feed content can be visible to other users through discovery and feed features. Messages are available to the conversation’s participants. Campus status is shown according to your selected audience and visibility controls, including blocking. Depending on those settings, students beyond your accepted friends may see your status, such as students at the same campus or university. Campus-name and zone visibility are controlled separately from audience access. Your optional major or specialisation is currently available to you in your own account settings, not displayed to other students. Private student-email information is used for verification. We also send information to the service providers described above to operate these features. We may disclose information if required by law, to protect users, or to investigate abuse and safety issues.

Profile Photos, Reports, And Moderation

Newly submitted profile photos are stored as private pending review items until they are manually approved. A pending submission does not replace your public profile picture. Once approved, a photo is published as an avatar with a public image URL; someone who has that URL may access or copy it outside the app. Reports and moderation records are available to authorised reviewers. Blocks restrict contact and visibility in the app, but cannot recall content another person has already received or copied.

Retention And Account Deletion

Account and social records are stored to provide the service while your account exists. Status or feed expiry changes what is shown as current; it does not mean every underlying record or notification has been erased. Signing out or uninstalling Veylo does not delete your account.

Open Profile > Settings > Delete account to start deletion. The password flow asks you to type DELETE, enter your current password, and confirm. If your sign-in method has no password, use Request deletion support on that screen. If you cannot access the app, use the public account-deletion page.

Successful account deletion removes the sign-in account, profile and saved study details, statuses, friend connections, your feed posts and comments, messages you sent, your chat memberships, notification tokens and preferences, reports you submitted, and photo submissions. It also removes the profile-photo files held in your account’s pending-photo and avatar storage folders. Deletion can fail or stop partway through; an error is not a confirmation that all these steps finished. Contact support if you cannot complete it.

Some records are not automatically erased with the account:

  • Feedback and course suggestions may remain after their account link is removed. Text you entered may still identify you.
  • Reports made by other people and moderation records may retain descriptions or references after account links are removed.
  • Shared conversation records and other participants’ messages remain. Notification records held for recipients may retain names, previews, or references even when no longer shown in their inbox.
  • A push-security record retains an app installation identifier, former account identifier, and a one-way binding proof to prevent delayed notification registration from attaching an installation to the wrong account. It is not erased when the notification token is deleted.

These remaining records have no fixed automatic deletion period. Service-provider logs, backups, support correspondence, and copies already delivered to other people are not all removed by the in-app deletion operation. Contact support to request review of remaining personal information or to ask what can be removed. Information that must be retained to meet a legal obligation may be excluded from deletion.
