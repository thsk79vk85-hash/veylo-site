Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Veylo collects, uses, stores, and protects information when students use the Veylo campus social app.

Information We Collect

Veylo may collect the following information when you use the app:

  • Account information, including email address and authentication identifiers.
  • Profile information, including name or public display name, university, campus, degree or course, year level, and student email verification status.
  • Campus status information, including general campus, zone, availability, status text, status notes, and status update times.
  • Social information, including friend requests, accepted friend connections, messages, feed posts, and related timestamps.
  • Device and technical information needed to operate, secure, debug, and improve the app.

Location And Campus Presence

Veylo is designed around campus-level presence. The app should show general campus zones or user-selected campus status rather than exact GPS coordinates to other users. If precise device location is used in future features such as geofencing, it will be used to determine general campus presence and not to publicly display exact coordinates.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide app functionality, including:

  • Creating and managing accounts.
  • Helping users choose university, campus, and degree details.
  • Showing campus status to accepted friends according to app privacy controls.
  • Supporting friend discovery, friend requests, messages, and campus feed posts.
  • Maintaining security, preventing abuse, debugging issues, and improving reliability.

Storage And Service Providers

Veylo uses Supabase for authentication, database storage, and related backend services. Supabase stores account, profile, social, status, feed, and message records needed for the app to function. We do not sell personal information.

Sharing

Veylo shares app content with other users only as needed for the social features you use, such as friend connections, feed posts, messages, and campus status visibility. We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect users, or to investigate abuse.

Analytics And Crash Reporting

Veylo does not currently include a separate third-party analytics or advertising SDK. Apple, Expo, Supabase, and operating system services may provide technical logs or diagnostics needed to operate, distribute, secure, or debug the app.

Account And Data Deletion

You can request account or data deletion by contacting support at John.tawadros@icloud.com. Include the email address associated with your Veylo account. We may need to retain limited records where required for security, abuse prevention, legal compliance, or backup integrity.

Age Restrictions

Veylo is intended for university students and is not directed to children under 13. Users must meet the minimum age required by applicable law and by Apple App Store rules in their region.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact John.tawadros@icloud.com.
