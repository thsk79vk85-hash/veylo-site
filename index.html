How it works

You already ask these questions. Veylo just makes the answer useful.

The app is built around quick campus status, trusted friends, lightweight updates, and getting from “maybe” to an actual plan faster.

01

Campus

See who is sharing that they are on campus.

Instead of sending five separate messages, open Campus and get a quick view of your friends’ current updates. You see their campus-level status—not a live map or exact pin.

  • Statuses like On campus, Studying, In class, and Leaving soon
  • General campus or zone information where someone chooses to share it
  • Friends-only visibility and invisible mode

Built to answer the privacy question before you need to ask it

Your exact location is never shown.

Veylo is about campus-level presence and the updates you choose to share. Your friends do not get a live GPS pin, and you can control whether you appear to be around at all.
