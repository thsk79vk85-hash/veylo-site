Read the full privacy policy ↗
Do my friends see an exact location?+

The campus-status feature shows general campus or zone information, rather than a live GPS pin. A zone can still tell someone which campus area you’re in.

How does location work?+

When you choose a location-based feature, your phone determines your campus presence. The normal status flow sends Veylo the resulting campus or zone and status time, rather than raw GPS coordinates. Automatic updates require background permission.

Can I stop sharing?+

You can turn off automatic tracking and revoke your phone’s location permission. That stops future checks; it doesn’t erase an existing status. You can update or hide your status using the app’s privacy controls.

What information does Veylo store?+

Veylo stores the account, social and campus-status information needed to run the service. What friends see is different from what the service stores. Read the policy for details, or contact support@veylo.app with access or privacy questions.
