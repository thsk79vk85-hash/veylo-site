Can friends see exactly where I am?+

They’ll see your campus or campus area, not a live pin of your exact location.

Can I hide my status?+

Yep. You can hide your campus status in the app’s privacy settings.

Who can see my campus status?+

Your accepted friends, based on your privacy settings.
