Delete your Veylo account

You can request deletion of your Veylo (Veylo Social) account and associated data without downloading the app or signing in.

Send a deletion request

Please contact us from the email address associated with your Veylo account so we can verify and process your request.

Email Veylo support

If the button does not open an email app, send an email to support@veylo.app with the subject “Veylo account deletion”. State that you want your account and associated data deleted. If you no longer have access to your account email, explain that in your request. Ownership verification may be needed. Do not send your password or sign-in codes.

Opening the email link does not send a request or delete an account. Send the email to submit your request. Email requests need manual processing; this page does not perform deletion or provide an automatic completion confirmation.

Already signed in?

Open Profile > Settings > Delete account. Type DELETE, enter your current password, and confirm. If your sign-in method has no password, use Request deletion support on the same screen. Signing out or uninstalling Veylo does not delete your account.

What your request covers

Successful account deletion removes the sign-in account, profile and study details, campus statuses, friend connections, your posts, comments and sent messages, chat memberships, notification tokens and preferences, your reports, and photo submissions. The deletion process also removes files in your account’s pending-photo and avatar storage folders.

Feedback and course suggestions can remain with the account link removed. Reports from other people, moderation records, shared conversation records, and recipients’ notification records can retain text or references. Other participants’ messages remain. A notification-security record retains the installation identifier, former account identifier, and one-way binding proof to prevent notifications being assigned to the wrong account. These remaining records have no fixed automatic deletion period.

Provider logs, backups, support emails, and copies already delivered to other people are not all erased by the in-app operation. Contact support to request review of remaining personal information. Information legally required to be kept may be excluded. See the Privacy Policy for details.

If in-app deletion shows an error, some steps may already have run. Do not treat the error as confirmation of complete deletion; retry or contact support. A manual deletion request is a request for processing, not confirmation that the account has already been removed.
