Veylo child safety standards

Last updated: 29 October 2026.

Veylo is operated by John Tawadros personally in Queensland, Australia. Veylo is for people aged 18 or older based in Australia. This eligibility rule does not guarantee independent verification of every account's age or remove our responsibility for children appearing in content or reports.

Responsible child-safety contact: John Tawadros, support@joinveylo.com. This contact also receives child-safety compliance concerns.

Prohibited conduct and material

Veylo prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation, including grooming, sexualised contact with a child, sexual extortion, trafficking, and creating, requesting, sharing or promoting child sexual abuse material. Do not use the service to possess such material. This includes computer-generated or manipulated material involving the sexual exploitation of children.

These standards apply to profiles, photos, statuses, group names, posts, comments, messages and support interactions. Veylo also prohibits harassment, threats, stalking, non-consensual intimate images, sexualised impersonation and pornography or sexually explicit material. Good-faith reports and lawful requests for help are allowed.

Report a concern

Use an available in-app reporting control or email support@joinveylo.com. Email is available without an account and if a form or target is unavailable. Give a username or lawful content location, approximate time and brief description.

Do not download, copy, forward or attach child sexual abuse material. You do not need to collect illegal material to report a concern. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes or recovery tokens.

We will provide an initial human response to safety complaints in less than 48 hours, including weekends. This begins or explains handling, not completion of an investigation, removal or resolution. It does not postpone urgent action required by law. These channels are not continuously monitored emergency services. For immediate danger in Australia call 000; you can also contact police or eSafety independently.

How concerns are handled

We will assess reports and take appropriate action to remove or disable access to child sexual abuse material when we become aware of it, and restrict accounts where justified. We will comply with applicable reporting, preservation and removal duties, including referrals to the Australian Federal Police where required and other appropriate child-protection or law-enforcement authorities. Reporting to one body does not necessarily discharge a duty to another. The initial-response target does not extend a legal reporting or removal deadline.

Any necessary evidence must be handled lawfully, with restricted access, a specific purpose and review of continued retention. Illegal material must not be routinely copied into support email or unnecessary logs, tickets or archives. We do not claim that every harmful item is automatically detected or screened before anyone sees it.

We will provide reasons and a review route where lawful while protecting reporters and others. We will not reveal a reporter's identity to the reported person without agreement unless required by law; incident details may still allow inference. A report is not malicious merely because it is not upheld. A restriction does not remove email appeal, privacy or deletion access.

Eligibility and review

Report a suspected under-18 account through the app or this contact. We will review it and take appropriate steps to restrict or close an ineligible account and address information that should no longer be held. An ordinary profile-photo review is a suitability review, not age or identity verification. Do not send a government ID or age-verification selfie; no such ordinary verification process is offered.

These standards do not replace duties under applicable child-safety law. See Terms, support and appeals, Privacy and account deletion.
